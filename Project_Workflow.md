# Sales and Customer Behaviour Analysis (SQL Capstone Project)

This project focuses on analyzing retail sales data to uncover actionable insights about customer behavior, sales trends, and business performance using SQL.

The dataset consists of 1000+ transactions across three branches (A, B, C) located in Yangon, Mandalay, and Naypyitaw. It includes information such as product lines, customer types, payment methods, timestamps, and revenue-related fields.

#🔧 Key Steps Performed
Data Wrangling
Created and structured SQL database and tables
Cleaned and handled missing values
Feature Engineering
Derived new columns like time of day, weekday, and month
Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)
Solved 28 business-related questions
Analyzed trends in sales, revenue, and customer behavior
#📊 Key Insights
Sales Analysis
Naypyitaw (Branch C) generated the highest revenue
January was the best-performing month, followed by March and February
Peak sales occur in the afternoon, especially on Mondays
Lowest activity observed during mornings
Customer Analysis
Total customers: 1000 (501 members, 499 normal)
Female customers dominate purchases in:
Food & Beverages
Fashion Accessories
Sports & Travel
Male customers lead in:
Electronics
Health & Beauty
Home & Lifestyle
#🚀 Business Recommendations
Target female customers for Food & Beverage promotions
Improve Health & Beauty marketing strategies
Run time-based campaigns:
Morning → Home & Lifestyle
Afternoon → Sports & Travel
Evening → Food & Beverages
Increase engagement and discounts during February
Implement city-specific marketing strategies based on customer demographics
